Kindle Delivery

How the EPUB gets to your Kindle

A

Send-to-Kindle Email (Recommended for v1)

Amazon assigns each Kindle a @kindle.com email. Send the EPUB as an attachment from an approved sender address.

Setup: Configure your sender email in the app settings + approve it in Amazon's "Manage Your Content and Devices"
Library: Python smtplib or aiosmtplib
Config: SMTP host/port/credentials + Kindle email address stored in env vars or a settings table

Pros

  • Works over WiFi, no local network needed
  • Battle-tested, Amazon handles conversion
  • Works with both stock Kindle and KOReader

Cons

  • Needs SMTP credentials configured
  • 50MB attachment limit
  • Slight delay (minutes)
B

KOReader OPDS Catalog

Serve an OPDS feed from the FastAPI backend. KOReader can browse and download EPUBs directly over your local network.

How: FastAPI serves an OPDS XML catalog at /opds listing available digests
KOReader: Add http://your-machine:8000/opds as a catalog source
Download: Browse digests on the Kindle, tap to download EPUB

Pros

  • No email config needed
  • Instant — no Amazon middleman
  • Browse and pick digests on the Kindle itself

Cons

  • Requires same local network
  • KOReader only (not stock Kindle)
  • OPDS spec is a bit verbose to implement

Both can coexist — recommend starting with one and adding the other later. Which do you want first?
